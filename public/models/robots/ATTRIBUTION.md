# Robot model attribution

The GLB files in this directory are browser-oriented conversions of complete URDF visual assemblies. Geometry is transformed and repackaged, not redrawn.

## Unitree

G1, H1, A1, Aliengo, B1, B2, Go1, Go2, and Laikago are converted from [`unitreerobotics/unitree_ros`](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros), revision `7d6075f7f58588b189b940130e3edab3c839b2df`, under the repository's BSD 3-Clause license. Copyright HangZhou YuShu TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD. / Unitree Robotics.

## Fourier

GR-1 is converted from [`FFTAI/Wiki-GRx-Models`](https://github.com/FFTAI/Wiki-GRx-Models), revision `7d96c758f048fe1bf92b3258864d94771ae0c093`, under GPL-3.0.

N1 is converted from the `FourierN1` branch of [`FFTAI/Wiki-GRx-Models`](https://github.com/FFTAI/Wiki-GRx-Models/tree/FourierN1), revision `f8e683f00d1d99deb882deb9dfce6030095b466a`, under Apache-2.0.

## AgiBot

The upstream [`AgibotTech/agibot_x1_train`](https://github.com/AgibotTech/agibot_x1_train) repository does not declare a software or model license. `agibot-x1.local.glb` is therefore excluded from version control and distribution. Developers may generate it locally from their own checkout with the explicit `--include-unlicensed` option.
